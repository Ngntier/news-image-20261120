## Lead — Shenzhen, the day after

- Trump, Xi and Putin held their first three-way meeting on Thursday
  19 November in Shenzhen (Kylin Villa, 81-90 minutes, Xi in the
  middle of a round table) and issued a four-point joint declaration:
  a nuclear war "cannot be won and must never be fought", plus a
  dialogue on nuclear risks and more refined petroleum supply. No
  binding arms deal, no timetable, no Ukraine settlement. (WSJ, CNN,
  Le Monde, Reuters via Open Chronicle)
- Reaction on 20 November: German Chancellor Friedrich Merz warned
  Europe would not accept a new Yalta-style settlement made without
  Europe; French President Emmanuel Macron made the same point; GOP
  senators condemned the meeting; Finland's President Stubb said the
  US urged removing the Ukraine clause from the declaration.

## Kyiv bridges hit overnight

- Russia launched a major overnight missile and drone attack on Kyiv,
  hitting three bridges across the Dnipro; at least three dead and
  about 15 injured (Mayor Klitschko). Russian strikes also damaged
  the Austrian and Dutch embassies in Kyiv. (Open Chronicle, RTE,
  Euronews)
- Ukrainian drones reportedly struck the Dorogobuzh chemical plant
  in Russia's Smolensk region; explosions and fire reported, damage
  unverified. (Kyiv Independent via Open Chronicle)

## Markets plunge in the AI rout (Friday 20 Nov)

- S&P 500 fell 3.7%, its seventh straight down day (longest losing
  streak since 2020); Nasdaq Composite fell 5.2%, its second-largest
  one-day points drop ever; Dow fell 1.9%, about 950 points. Both
  S&P 500 and Dow entered correction territory (10%+ off highs).
  (Investopedia, WSJ, Reuters)
- Nvidia fell 14% on Friday (down a third in two weeks since Jensen
  Huang's departure as CEO; down 23% on the week). Anthropic (ANT)
  fell about 9% to around $88-89, a day after its record Nasdaq debut
  (IPO $83, opened $111.50, closed $97.17, +17%).
- Asia: Nikkei 225 down 5.06%, Kospi down 5.21%, Hang Seng down
  2.24%; MSCI world index fell for a seventh straight session.

## Cyclone Akvan hits Odisha

- Cyclone Akvan crossed the north Andhra Pradesh-south Odisha coast
  near Gopalpur between 02:00 and 03:00 UTC on 20 November, winds
  70-80 km/h gusting to 90 km/h. More than 300,000 people evacuated
  to shelters; 9,341 shelters, 423 rescue teams; no deaths or major
  damage in the first assessment. Patrapur recorded 209 mm of rain.
  (IMD bulletins, The Watchers, Hindustan Times)

## ICJ Myanmar ruling: the day after

- The ICJ ruled on Thursday 19 November that Myanmar committed
  genocide against the Rohingya in 2017, the court's first such
  finding against a state; it ordered reparations and preventive
  measures. On 20 November the reaction and enforcement debate
  dominated coverage: over a million Rohingya remain displaced,
  mostly in Bangladesh. (ICJ, Open Chronicle, One Turn)
